Common Questions

How do I export my time logs?

In YOYO Log, tap the export button in your logs view. Your data exports as a clean CSV file.

Will my data sync across devices?

Yes! YOYO Log uses iCloud sync. Your logs automatically appear on all your devices.

Can I delete my account?

You can disable sync in settings, which removes your data from iCloud. Contact us for data deletion assistance.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted end-to-end and stored only in your iCloud account. We never see it.
