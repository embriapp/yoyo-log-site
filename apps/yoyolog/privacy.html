Privacy Policy

Last updated:

In short: YOYO Log only stores what you log. No tracking, no analytics, no selling data.

What We Collect

YOYO Log stores only what you create: your time tracking sessions, breaks, and jobs. We also store your display name (if you provide one) for your exported reports.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your data syncs privately through iCloud CloudKit:

  • Encrypted end-to-end — Your data is encrypted before leaving your device
  • Under your control — Only you can access your data through your Apple ID
  • No server access — We never see or store your data on our servers
  • Automatic backups — iCloud keeps your data safe across your devices

What We Don't Do

  • No tracking — We don't track your usage patterns or behavior
  • No analytics — We don't collect data about how you use the app
  • No ads — We don't serve ads or allow third-party advertising
  • No selling data — We don't sell, share, or distribute your information
  • No profiling — We don't build profiles based on your data

Authentication

YOYO Log uses your Apple ID for authentication only. We don't store your Apple ID credentials. Apple handles all authentication securely on their servers.

When You Export

When you export your time logs, YOYO Log creates a file on your device. We don't receive, store, or transmit this exported data.

Third-Party Services

YOYO Log relies on:

  • Apple iCloud — For secure cloud storage and sync
  • Apple App Store — For distribution and payments

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email us at embriapp@gmail.com

Privacy is fundamental. We design YOYO Log to protect it by default—not as an afterthought.
