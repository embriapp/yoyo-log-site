Privacy Policy

Last updated:

In short: We don't track you. We don't sell your data. Your notes are yours.

What We Collect

Brote stores only what you create: your notes, posts, and the content you choose to save. We also store your display name (if you provide one) for your exported documents.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your notes sync privately through iCloud CloudKit. This means:

  • Encrypted end-to-end — Your notes are encrypted before leaving your device
  • Under your control — Only you can access your data through your Apple ID
  • No Embri access — We never see or store your notes on our servers
  • Backed up automatically — iCloud keeps your data safe across your devices

What We Don't Do

  • No tracking — We don't track your usage, behavior, or analytics
  • No analytics — We don't collect data about how you use Brote
  • No ads — We don't serve ads or allow third-party advertising
  • No selling data — We don't sell, share, or distribute your information
  • No profiling — We don't build profiles based on your content

Authentication

Brote uses your Apple ID for authentication only. We don't store your Apple ID credentials. Apple handles all authentication securely on their servers.

When You Export

When you export your notes, Brote creates a file on your device. We don't receive, store, or transmit this exported data.

Third-Party Services

Brote relies on:

  • Apple iCloud — For secure cloud storage and sync
  • Apple App Store — For distribution and payment (Brote is free)

Please review Apple's privacy policy for details on their data handling practices.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we'll notify you within the app. Continued use of Brote means you accept the updated terms.

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email us at embriapp@gmail.com

Your privacy is fundamental. We design Brote to protect it by default — not as an afterthought.
