Privacy Policy

Last updated:

In short: Brote does not have a server, does not collect your data, and does not share your data with anyone — including us. Everything you enter is stored on your device and, if you enable it, synced across your own devices through your personal iCloud account.

What Information Brote Handles

Brote stores the information you choose to enter, including:

  • Account names, balances, and types
  • Transactions, categories, and notes
  • Budgets, envelopes, and savings goals
  • Recurring bill information

This information is used solely to power the app's features (tracking, budgeting, reporting) on your own device.

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device, in a local database.
  • In your personal iCloud account, if iCloud sync is available and signed in, using Apple's CloudKit service. This lets your data sync between your own devices (e.g., iPhone and iPad). Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We (the developer of Brote) do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to your data. There is no backend that transmits your financial information anywhere.

Receipt Scanning and Screenshot Import

When you scan a receipt or import transactions from a screenshot, Brote uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text from the image. This processing happens entirely on your device — images and extracted text are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Face ID / Passcode

If you enable app-lock, Brote uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to verify your identity via Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Brote never has access to your biometric data — this is handled entirely by Apple's Secure Enclave.

Notifications

Brote may send you local notifications (e.g., budget alerts, bill reminders, weekly/monthly spending summaries). These are generated and scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any push notification server operated by us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers. Brote contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data Deletion

You can delete any account, transaction, budget, envelope, or goal at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data; if iCloud sync was enabled, you can also delete synced data via Settings on your device (Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage).

Children's Privacy

Brote is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Brote's data practices change (for example, if we add bank account linking or any server-based feature in the future), this policy will be updated accordingly before that feature is released.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at embriapp@gmail.com

Your privacy is fundamental. We design Brote to protect it by default — not as an afterthought.
