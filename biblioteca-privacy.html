Biblioteca Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Biblioteca is an app for reading your own PDF books and listening to your own audiobooks. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: Biblioteca does not collect your data, and nothing you read or listen to leaves your device except through your own iCloud account.

Information we collect

Biblioteca does not collect personal information. The developer receives no data from the app. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Your books and audiobooks

The PDF books and audio files you import stay on your device, in the app's storage. Biblioteca does not upload them anywhere. Your highlights, notes, collections, bookmarks, and page marks are also stored only on your device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Biblioteca saves your reading position (the page you're on) in your own iCloud account, so it can continue on your other devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's services. The developer cannot see it. You can turn off iCloud for the app in Settings.

Audiobook page matching

When you match pages to an audiobook, Biblioteca converts the narration to text on your device using an on-device speech model. The audio is not sent to the developer or any other company. The first time you use this feature, the app downloads the speech model from the internet. That download contains no information about you or your books.

Microphone

If you use the narration recording feature, Biblioteca uses the microphone to record your voice. Recordings are stored on your device with your book and are never sent anywhere.

Third parties

Biblioteca does not share information with third parties. Purchases, if any, are handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Biblioteca does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
